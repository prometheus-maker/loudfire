# Privacy

Loudfire collects nothing.

- It does nothing until you move its slider on a tab. Then it routes that tab's audio and video players through a volume
  control in your browser.
- If you tick "Remember for this site", it stores that site's name and your level in the add-on's local storage, on your
  computer. Untick it and both are deleted.
- No analytics, no tracking, no account, no ads, no network requests.

Permissions: `activeTab` and `scripting` (the tab you use it on), `storage` (your remembered sites). When you tick
"Remember for this site", Firefox asks you to allow that one site, so the level can come back on your next visit.
